# Sudoku v2 — drafting folio direction package

Scope: only `sudoku`. The puzzle, givens, solution, cell input, mistake count,
move count, completion rule, and score formula are frozen. Meowdoku is a
regression neighbor, not part of this visual redesign.

## Experience pillars

1. **A puzzle worth marking by hand.** Every tap should feel like selecting,
   pencilling, correcting, or stamping a physical architect's folio.
2. **Quiet logic, legible consequence.** Routine play remains calm; an error,
   completed 3×3 block, and completed board must be distinguishable without
   relying on color or text alone.
3. **Earned drafting ceremony.** The brass compass and crossed indigo pencils
   are a real object in the interface, not a victory-only sticker.

## Art pillars

1. Warm ivory layered paper, graphite/indigo ink, and restrained brass.
2. Squared registration marks and drafting-board construction rather than soft
   generic mobile cards.
3. One generated signature family—the compass/pencil medallion—supported by
   code-native folio, grid, marks, and motion.

## Anti-pillars

1. No neon casino glow, glossy sci-fi panels, or generic dark dashboard.
2. No generated text, digits, grid, or gameplay-critical information.
3. No particle fog that obscures a cell or makes every input feel terminal.

## Visual grammar and hierarchy

- **Metaphor:** an architect's drafting folio on a quiet desk.
- **Stable signature beat:** GAG compass/pencil medallion at the game header,
  approximately 39×39 px, visible in ordinary play.
- **Board:** layered ivory paper, dark slate grid, alternating block papers,
  brass registration corners, and clear printed-vs-entered number hierarchy.
- **Controls:** warm paper keycaps; numeric copy uses `NUMBER_FONT`, CJK uses
  `UI_FONT`. Generated pixels never carry copy.
- **Result:** the same medallion and paper vocabulary close the interaction arc.

## Semantic feedback ladder

| Grade | Event | Object-local response | Broader response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| intent | select cell | press/rebound, registration focus | quiet paper-select sound, short haptic |
| 1 | correct / erase | digit scale or graphite erase mark | ink/paper sound, tiny haptic |
| 2 | wrong digit | horizontal cell shake and red-pencil correction | scratch sound, short double haptic |
| 3 | finish 3×3 block | block wash and 76 px compass stamp | brass stamp sound, three-part haptic |
| 4 | finish board | 122 px compass ceremony and board-wide settle | authored GAG completion sound, strongest bounded haptic, result folio |

The exact particles, arcs, timing, and labels are presentation choices, not a
rigid template. Grade separation is mandatory; a specific effect primitive is
not.

## GAG use and shipping boundary

- Semantic image and audio search ran first; retrieved generic candidates were
  rejected for lacking the drafting identity.
- The selected visual was generated by Gemini through GAG pure-API mode,
  background-removed through Remove.bg, then trimmed to a runtime PNG.
- The selected completion cue was generated by ElevenLabs through GAG and
  loudness-normalized into runtime OGG.
- Source masters and rejected candidates remain in the HOME-WSL GAG archive.
  Only the two derived runtime resources ship in this repository.
- Exact prompts, hashes, rejection reasons, and runtime references are in
  `sudoku-v2.gag-asset-ledger.json`.

## Acceptance

- Baseline, stable state, intent/impact/settle/recovery captures, error, block,
  completion, result, and a continuous completion sequence.
- Authoritative state snapshots prove immutable givens and unchanged move,
  mistake, completion, and score rules.
- CJK/font gates, all-game regression, clean Web export, PCK resource scan,
  browser console/request checks, performance trace, exact deployed hashes,
  retained prior pack, and one real online Sudoku move.

## Non-goals

- No puzzle generator, hint system, difficulty redesign, scoring change, screen
  navigation change, or catalog-wide reskin.
